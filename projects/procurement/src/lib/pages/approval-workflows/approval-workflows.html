Approval Workflows

Configure multi-level approval chains for procurement documents

ℹ️ A workflow routes a document (e.g. a purchase order) through an ordered chain of approval steps. The workflow is chosen by document type and by the document's value falling within its amount range (Min–Max); the default workflow with the highest priority wins when several match. Each step can require one or more approvals from a role — mark a step parallel to let its approvers act simultaneously rather than in sequence, and optional to allow it to be skipped. If a step is not actioned within its escalation window (in days), it is escalated to the next approver.
