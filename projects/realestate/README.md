# @nexcore/realestate

The Real Estate app: property and land, projects and inventory, listings and leads, bookings,
collections, transfers and possession, leasing and estate management, societies and facilities,
construction and turnkey builds, project finance and compliance, and the customer portal.

Four lines of business are switchable configuration — Brokerage, Development, Contracting and
Estate Management — so an agency, a plot developer, a turnkey builder and a managing agent each
see only the screens their business actually uses.
