How fiscal years work
  • Edit is allowed only while a year is open and has no transactions. Once journal entries are posted within its dates, the year is locked.
  • A year that contains transactions cannot be deleted — only years with no activity can be removed.
  • Closing a year makes it read-only (no edit or delete). You can Reopen it later if you need to make changes.
