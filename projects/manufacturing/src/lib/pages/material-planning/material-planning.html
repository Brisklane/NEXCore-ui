Planning Parameters Guide
Reorder Point When available stock drops to or below this number, MRP triggers a planned order. Example: Wooden Table = 10 means order more when fewer than 10 are on hand.
Safety Stock A buffer held above demand to absorb unexpected delays or spikes. MRP adds this to the net requirement. Example: 2 units means always keep at least 2 extra.
Lot Size Minimum order quantity — MRP rounds planned orders up to the nearest multiple. Example: Lot Size = 5 means you order in batches of 5, never 3 or 7.
Lead Time (days) Days from placing an order to receiving it. Used to back-schedule: if a table is needed June 10 and lead time is 7 days, the order must be placed by June 3.
Planning Horizon (days) How far ahead MRP looks for demand. Set to 60 to only plan the next 2 months. Leave at 0 to consider all future demand with no cutoff.
Scrap % Expected production loss rate. MRP inflates planned qty to compensate. Example: Need 100 tables at 5% scrap → MRP plans 106 so 100 good ones remain after rejects.
